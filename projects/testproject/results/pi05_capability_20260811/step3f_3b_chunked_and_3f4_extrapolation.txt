# 3f.3b — CHUNKED offload supersedes 3f.3's bulk-transfer figures
#          and 3f.4 — capacity extrapolation. Measured 2026-08-14.

CHUNKED TRANSFER (state on CPU, streamed through a 1 GB pinned buffer)
  state size       time     effective
          2 GB    0.55s         7.2 GB/s
          8 GB    2.00s         8.0 GB/s
         16 GB    3.96s         8.1 GB/s
  chunked round-trip   8.0 GB/s
  single bulk pinned  56.6 GB/s   <- what 3f.3's +29.4% assumed

  8-bit state  +2.07 s/step = +197%   (3f.3 said +29.4%)
  fp32  state  +8.24 s/step = +785%   (arithmetic said +112%)
  => a 7 h run becomes ~21 h, NOT ~9 h.
  => +197% falls inside the literature's 1.93-4.28x for real ZeRO-Offload;
     +29.4% did not. The optimistic figure assumed a transfer a real
     implementation cannot perform.
