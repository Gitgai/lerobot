★ TWO INDEPENDENT DEFECTS HAD TO BE FIXED, AND NEITHER SHOWS UP IN TRAINING
  1 torch      Optimizer.load_state_dict casts state to the PARAM's dtype and
               device (torch/optim/optimizer.py:754). fp32 host -> bf16 cuda.
               SILENT — the toy round-trip "succeeded" with both invariants
               destroyed. Fixed in CPUOffloadAdamW.load_state_dict.
  2 accelerate AcceleratedOptimizer.__init__ does move_to_device(state, device)
               (accelerate/optimizer.py:69-75), hauling all 26 GB onto the card
               at prepare(). Fixed via prepare(device_placement=[..,False,..]).

  ⇒ Defect 2 is INVISIBLE on a fresh run: the state dict is empty at prepare()
    time, so the move is a no-op. It fires ONLY on resume. The fresh 60-step
    run passed TWICE while this bug was live.
  ⇒ This is the concrete proof of the standing rule: a rung is a POC until it
    CHECKPOINTS AND RESUMES. Step time and loss would never have revealed it.
