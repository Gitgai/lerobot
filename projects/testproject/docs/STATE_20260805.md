## 5. GR00T N1.7 — DONE, and it broke an assumption

```text
GR00T N1.7 now runs in the sim on a VERIFIED serving path. Getting there meant
fixing THREE adapter bugs: UNITS (the checkpoint speaks LeRobot motor units,
not the sim's radians - LeIsaac's own client does this conversion and a raw ZMQ
client bypasses it), RELATIVE ACTIONS (arm output is a delta; only the gripper
is absolute), and AN EXTRA CAMERA (it declares front+wrist; the S2 `top` view
was still being forwarded). The earlier "grasp=TRUE" was those bugs.

RESULT: reaches the grasp frame to 0.039 m, closes, predicate TRUE for 80
consecutive steps - AND THE ORANGE MOVES 0.0001 m. It acquires nothing.

*** AND THE INSTRUCTION DOMINATES ***
  "pick up the orange..."  -> retreats to 0.394 m and freezes
  "Grab pens and place..." -> approaches and closes
Same checkpoint, same scene, ONLY THE SENTENCE CHANGED.
CAREFUL: this is instruction SENSITIVITY, not "match the training string".
The checkpoint is robocurve/gr00t-n1.7-so101-molmoact2 - a BROAD community
fine-tune (2,242 eps / 1.8M frames / 39 repos) whose card publishes NO
instruction strings. Neither sentence we tried is a verified training string,
and the winning one names PENS in a scene full of ORANGES. Likely the model
keys on the PHRASING PATTERN "Grab X and place into Y". Untested hypothesis.

*** THE GROUND-TRUTH TRAP ***
mdp.orange_grasped = (dist < 0.05) AND (gripper < 0.60). PROXIMITY AND CLOSURE.
It does NOT test contact, force or lift. Closing on air next to the orange
scores TRUE. Our sim GT was weaker than assumed - the sim twin of the real-arm
lesson behind the finger-stall test. Every sim "grasp" in this project must be
read as proximity+closure unless OBJECT DISPLACEMENT is also reported.
```
